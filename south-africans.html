Why We Understand You

🇬🇧 English

We have extensive experience teaching Dutch to South Africans whose first language is Afrikaans and/or English.

Our tutors understand the unique challenges South Africans face when learning Dutch. They know which aspects of the language typically require less attention and which language skills need extra focus.

For Afrikaans speakers, reading and listening comprehension in Dutch are often relatively easy to master, while speaking and writing usually present greater challenges. That is why we provide targeted support focused specifically to these needs.

We also share a wide range of practical tips to make the learning process easier, including guidance on the correct pronunciation of Dutch sounds and words, as well as support with accurate sentence structure and natural language use.

🇿🇦 Afrikaans

Ons het uitgebreide ervaring in die onderrig van Nederlands aan Suid-Afrikaners wie se moedertaal Afrikaans en/of Engels is.

Ons dosente verstaan die unieke uitdagings waarmee Suid-Afrikaners te doen kry wanneer hulle Nederlands aanleer. Ons dosente weet presies watter aspekte van die taal minder aandag verg en watter spesifieke taalvaardighede ekstra aandag benodig.

Vir Afrikaanssprekendes is Nederlandse lees- en luistervaardighede dikwels relatief maklik om te bemeester, terwyl skryf en praat gewoonlik groter uitdagings inhou. Daarom bied ons doelgerigte ondersteuning wat spesifiek op hierdie behoeftes fokus.

Ons deel ook ’n wye verskeidenheid praktiese wenke om die leerproses te vergemaklik, insluitend leiding oor die korrekte uitspraak van Nederlandse klanke en woorde, asook ondersteuning met korrekte sinskonstruksie en natuurlike taalgebruik.

Key insight for Afrikaans speakers

Reading & Listening vs. Writing & Speaking

For Afrikaans speakers, reading and listening in Dutch are often surprisingly accessible — but writing and speaking require dedicated, targeted practice. Our tutors adjust the curriculum accordingly, from day one.

What We Focus On for You

Because we know your starting point, we can skip what you don’t need and concentrate on what matters most.

🧩

Afrikaans–Dutch similarities

You already have a significant vocabulary advantage. We build on your Afrikaans foundation from the very first session, making progress feel natural and fast.

🗣️

Speaking confidence

Spoken Dutch differs considerably from Afrikaans. We dedicate targeted time to fluency, rhythm, and natural conversational flow so you sound confident, not translated.

✍️

Writing accuracy

Dutch spelling, grammar, and sentence structure diverge from Afrikaans in important ways. We focus on the rules that trip up South Africans most — efficiently.

🔊

Dutch pronunciation

The Dutch “g”, long vowels, and diphthongs are common stumbling blocks. We provide targeted pronunciation exercises designed specifically for South African speakers.

🇳🇱

Dutch workplace & culture

Understanding Dutch directness, social norms, and professional communication makes integration smoother. We weave culture into every lesson.

🤝

One dedicated tutor

You work with one tutor throughout your programme. They get to know your background, track your progress, and tailor every lesson to where you are right now.
