Privacy Policy

What information we collect, why we use it, and the choices you have over your personal data.

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Panoramic Learning offers online Dutch and English lessons. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Panoramic Learning is operated by HJSIEM LLC, a United States limited liability company based in Dallas, Texas, United States. Our administration and business operations are conducted in the United States. We provide online lessons to students who may be located in Europe and other countries.

You can contact us at judith@panoramic-learning.com or +1 608 738 9747.

What we collect

When you complete our booking form or contact us, we collect the information you choose to provide. This may include your:

  • Name and email address
  • Telephone or WhatsApp number
  • Company and billing address
  • Language background
  • Preferred lesson times
  • Learning goals
  • Messages to us

Only your name and email address are required through our current booking form.

Once you begin lessons, we may also keep scheduling information, attendance records, progress notes, and test results.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your payment information is submitted directly to Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number, although we keep payment confirmations and invoices.

Our website host and technical providers may automatically collect standard information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and security logs.

Please do not include sensitive personal information, such as medical information, passwords, or payment-card details, in the message box.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Arrange and provide lessons
  • Match you with a tutor
  • Tailor your learning programme
  • Process payments and invoices
  • Communicate with you about your lessons
  • Operate and protect our website and services
  • Meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we generally process your information because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to provide the lessons or services you purchased. We may also process information to comply with legal obligations or for legitimate business interests such as administering our services, maintaining security, and responding to enquiries.

We rely on consent where applicable, including for optional marketing where consent is required.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not use it to create advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

Where necessary, we may share relevant information with:

  • Your tutor
  • Our website host and technical providers
  • Stripe, for payment processing
  • Communication or video-meeting providers used to provide lessons
  • Our bookkeeper, accountant, or professional advisers
  • Government authorities where disclosure is legally required

Our tutors receive only the information reasonably needed to teach and communicate with you.

Some service providers process information on our behalf, while others may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

International processing and transfers

Panoramic Learning is based and administered in the United States. If you are located outside the United States, your personal information may therefore be transferred to or processed in the United States. Information may also be processed in other countries where our tutors or service providers are located.

Where applicable data-protection law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will use appropriate safeguards.

Cookies

We do not use analytics, advertising, or marketing cookies.

Our website host and security providers may use cookies or similar technologies that are strictly necessary to operate, protect, and maintain the website.

How long we keep your information

We generally keep:

  • Student records while you study with us and for two years afterwards
  • Enquiries that do not result in lessons for approximately one year
  • Invoices, payment confirmations, and tax records for as long as reasonably necessary to meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements

We may keep information longer where legally required or necessary to resolve a dispute. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

Your choices

You may contact judith@panoramic-learning.com to ask us to:

  • Provide a copy of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete information we no longer need
  • Restrict or object to certain uses of your information
  • Stop sending marketing messages

We may be unable to delete information that we are legally required to keep, such as certain invoices and tax records.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under local law. People in the European Union or European Economic Area may also have rights to data portability, to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and to complain to the data-protection authority in the country where they live or work.

Security

We limit access to student information to people who reasonably need it, use reputable providers, and protect our accounts using appropriate security measures.

No online system is completely secure. If a security incident occurs, we will notify affected individuals and relevant authorities where required by law.

Children

A parent or legal guardian must make the booking and provide the necessary information for any student under 18.

We do not knowingly ask children under 13 to submit personal information directly through this website. Where information about a child under 13 is needed to provide lessons, we seek to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide the service and to involve the parent or legal guardian as required by applicable law, including the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) where it applies.

We do not condition a child’s participation on providing more personal information than is reasonably necessary for the relevant lesson or service. We take reasonable steps to protect children’s information and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law.

If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information without appropriate parental involvement or consent, please contact us. Where required, we will take appropriate steps to delete the information.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy if our services, providers, or information-handling practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
