What We Offer

What You Can Expect From Us

Everything we do is tailored to help your international employees thrive in the Netherlands.

🎯

Tailor-made programmes

Aligned with role, sector, and individual learning objectives. No generic lessons — every programme is designed around your team’s specific needs.

🖥️

Online lessons

Private lessons via Google Meet. No travel, no classroom coordination. Your employees learn from wherever they work.

🕐

Flexible scheduling

Suited for international teams and time zones. Lessons available any day between 5:00 and 24:00 CET, seven days a week.

💬

Experienced native tutors

All our tutors are native Dutch speakers with international and expat backgrounds. They understand the challenges your employees face firsthand.

🏢

Focus on workplace culture

Language lessons include Dutch workplace culture, professional communication norms, and the unwritten rules of working in the Netherlands.

🌐

Language & culture integration

Combining language and cultural knowledge leads to faster and more sustainable integration, benefiting both the employee and your organisation.

🤝

Continuity & personal guidance

Each participant works with one dedicated tutor throughout their programme. Consistent relationships mean consistent, measurable progress.

Working Together

Practical Arrangements

Clear, professional agreements so you can focus on your people, not the paperwork.

1

Contract with the company

A clear service agreement between Panoramic Learning and your organisation, outlining scope, pricing, and responsibilities.

2

House rules signed by the student

Each participant receives and signs our house rules, ensuring shared expectations for punctuality, preparation, and engagement.

3

Monthly invoicing

Simple, transparent monthly invoices sent directly to your organisation. No surprises, no complicated billing structures.

4

Optional: quarterly progress reports

Receive detailed progress reports for each participant every quarter, giving HR and managers clear insight into development and next steps.

5

Regular evaluation meetings with HR / managers

We schedule regular check-ins with your HR team or managers to review progress, adjust the programme, and ensure alignment with your goals.
